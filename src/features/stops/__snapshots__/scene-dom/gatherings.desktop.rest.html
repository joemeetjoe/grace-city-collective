Gatherings

Every Sunday, somewhere near you.

House Churches

Every Sunday · 10:30 a.m. · five homes across West Georgia

12–20 people around a lay elder/pastor: singing, Scripture, prayer, communion, and teaching that takes the week seriously — then lunch, and the slow work of knowing one another. Children stay in the room.

All-Church Gathering

First Sunday of the month · the five rooms become one

The whole family in one place for a love feast: prayer, Scripture, singing, communion and teaching — casual, informal, participatory, and more like a family gathering than a service.
